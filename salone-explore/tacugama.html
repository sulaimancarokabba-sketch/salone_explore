Tacugama Sanctuary

Wildlife. Conservation. Hope.

Visit Sierra Leone's famous chimpanzee sanctuary and get close to nature. A perfect place for wildlife lovers and anyone who cares about conservation.

Tacugama Chimpanzee Sanctuary
Freetown, Sierra Leone
Freetown, Sierra Leone

About This Destination

Tacugama is a well-known wildlife sanctuary and a home to rescued chimpanzees and other animals. The sanctuary plays an important role in conservation, education and research. Visitors can learn about the amazing work being done to protect wildlife in Sierra Leone.

Things to Do

  • ✓See rescued chimpanzees up close
  • ✓Learn about wildlife conservation
  • ✓Visit the nature trails
  • ✓Take amazing photos
