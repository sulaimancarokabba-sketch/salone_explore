Salone Explore

More Than a Destination, It's an Experience

Salone Explore is a tourism website created to help people discover the beauty, culture and amazing places of Sierra Leone. We provide simple and useful information to make your travel planning easy and enjoyable.

Our Story

Sierra Leone is a country full of natural beauty, rich culture and warm people. From beautiful beaches and mountains to historical sites and vibrant communities, there is so much to explore. However, many people find it hard to get accurate and up-to-date information about places to visit, activities, accommodation and travel tips.

That's why we created Salone Explore — a simple, friendly and reliable platform that brings all this information together in one place. Our goal is to help both local and international visitors discover the best of Sierra Leone.
